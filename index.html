Understanding how word meaning is constructed

The purpose of this task is to examine how word meaning is constructed. The underlying hypothesis is that word meaning is constructed in the moment as a function of local context, i.e. surrounding words and sentences, in addition to one's own mental schema and other long-term memories. Here we try to simplify the surrounding context by reducing it to a handful of words in the absence of grammatical structure. By doing so, we hope to isolate the fundamental dynamics governing word meaning construction.

Determining what word is currently being attended

Given the comparatively large number of stimuli presented simultaneously, is it possible to determine which words are being considered at any given moment? In the actual experiment, mouse pointer position would be tracked in combination with eye position, but this visualization is meant to show that mouse pointer position alone often allows decent determination of which word is being considered at a given moment.
