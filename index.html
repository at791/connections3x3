This game records how you play (which words you point at and click, and how long you take) to study how people solve puzzles. Nothing that identifies you is collected.

How to play

The nine words make three groups of three - select three words that belong together and press Submit. You can make three mistakes. Every three puzzles make a level. Play as many as you like, and press End session when you're done.
